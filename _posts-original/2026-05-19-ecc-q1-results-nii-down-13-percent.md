ECC’s Q1 2026 results were more in line with expectations than OXLC’s results.

·       NII of $0.20 was $0.01 below consensus expectation




·       Future quarterly dividend of $0.18 is declared through Q3 2026




·       Current Stock price is $4.03, up $0.06 or 15bps




·       Dividend coverage is strong NII/Dividend (111.1%) and Recurring $ Flows/Dividend (261.1%), both historically strong.




·       ECC’s underlying loan Market Value down from 97% in Q4 2025 to 95% Q1 2026 is a positive sign but WA loan spread is down a bit.




Tom Majewski was much more positive in this conference call than the last two.  I had some problems with my reception on the call but he was upbeat on loan spreads, CLO equity market opening up, and he noted their look through default rate was 32bps compared to the leverage loan market default rate of 1.4% (this only includes bankruptcy or non-payment defaults and doesn’t include distressed exchanges, which are much more frequent.  I have read the leverage loan default rate with distressed exchanges is more like 4%- 5%).




Once ECC publishes its conference call transcript, I will add to this post.
