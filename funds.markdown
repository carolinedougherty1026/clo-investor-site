## Education: Understanding CLO Fund Structures

CLO funds invest in different layers of collateralized loan obligations, each carrying distinct risk and return characteristics. Equity-focused funds typically offer higher potential returns but are more sensitive to underlying loan performance and market conditions.

Debt-focused funds generally provide more stable income streams with varying levels of credit risk depending on tranche positioning. Understanding these distinctions is essential when evaluating performance, income potential, and overall portfolio fit.

**Equity Tranches** — Higher yield potential, greater sensitivity to default cycles and refinancing dynamics.

**Debt Tranches** — More stable income, with risk varying meaningfully by rating and seniority.
